## Part-Time Editor (10 hrs/week, $500/month)

**About TruthWorldNews:**
We're the cynical antidote to boring mainstream media. AI-assisted, human-reviewed, brutally honest tech journalism.

**Role:**
Review AI-generated drafts for voice consistency, fact-check claims, and ensure every article meets our editorial standards.

**Requirements:**
- Native English speaker
- Deep knowledge of tech industry (AI, crypto, startups)
- Sarcastic writing style (show us your best snark)
- Available 10 hours/week, flexible schedule
- Comfortable with Google Docs, Slack, GitHub

**Responsibilities:**
- Review 20 articles/week in admin dashboard
- Maintain "Zane Edge" voice consistency
- Fact-check claims against original sources
- Write editorial notes for significant changes
- Flag articles for correction or rejection

**To Apply:**
Send 3 writing samples + a 200-word rant about any tech hype cycle to [email].
